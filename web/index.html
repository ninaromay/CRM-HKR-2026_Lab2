A little about me...

I'm a creative and versatile professional with a background in programming, web development and 3D art. Over the years, I've worked with technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React and WordPress while also developing my skills in digital art and 3D creation. These experiences have taught me to combine technical problem-solving with creativity and visual thinking. Today, I'm taking my experience in a new direction by studying to become a Spanish teacher at upper-secondary level. I want to combine my technical and creative background with my passion for languages and education, creating engaging ways for students to learn and express themselves. My goal is to continue learning, building and teaching — bringing together technology, creativity and communication.
