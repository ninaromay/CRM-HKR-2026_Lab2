Burger Jazz

Burger Jazz is a real restaurant chain, located in Spain, open in several locations across different areas of Madrid and Barcelona. I worked with the restaurant owner and his assistant to create this very unique website from scratch. For this I used ReactJS, a JavaScript framework, and Three.js, a specialised library which has for purpose to import, display and control 3D graphics. We spent a few months, changing design several times, perfecting the mobile version and finding a suitable host. The web sadly was not utilised once finished, thus it stays as my personal project and was deployed in Netlify: Burger-Jazz.

Disclaimer: I do not own the current official Burger Jazz website nor I am linked to the company anymore. The project is kept entirely to be displayed as a skill showcase.

Gleechi Hands

Gleechi is a real company, located in Sweden, which works towards the creation of VR trainings within different areas of expertise. I worked with them for 6 months. I created this hands using Z-brush, did retopology, UV Mapping and material recreation in 3Ds-Max, texturing in Substance Painter.

The hands are still in use today by the company.
